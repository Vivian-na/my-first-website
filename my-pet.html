Meet Peanut, My Cat!

Peanut was just a name we came up, which was perfectly suitable her with her white, orange and black fur on her body. Peanut was a wonderful cat who came to live with us while she was just a small kitten. She was so jovial and playful. I remember on the first day she came, she ran up and down under the sofas, scratch or play with people's feet as she bit on them and then she would run to hide under the sofa which was a sign that she always wanted to play. Because she was so young and didn't have a mother to feed her, we bought her milk and a nursing or feeding bottle, where she'd chew them instead of just drinking her milk. Although she might have scratched and bitten some people, she never did it harmfully enough to leave scars, but it was because of her playfulness. Over time, she grew up so fast and as time went she slowly stopped being playful and jovial as she was but she still enjoyed different people's company and sometimes playing.

  3. She loves playing and going for walks.

What Peanut Loved & How She behaved.

Peanut loved treats, cuddling into people and going for walks. She loved treats probably more than she loved most people. It actually got to a point where she only ate treats and wouldn't even look at homemade food except for proteins obviously. She was so spoiled. Also, when people she knew came, she'd lie on their laps or even play with them. But when it came to people she did not know, it was a whole different story. For example, some technicians once came over to check out our metre box, as they stood at the door talking to my mother, Peanut stood on her two legs arms tucked forward and peeked at them at the corner of one of the sofas. I remember informing my sister and we laughed but Peanut was very unbothered, just curious who those men were. Once they finished their inspection and chat with my mother for sometime, she then got down on all her limbs and continued walking like nothing happened. This is one of the funiest things I remember about her.

What happened to Peanut.

Once when I was randomly wandering around the house, I saw Peanut coming in through the kitchen window. I was not suprised at the thought that she was able to jump by walking through the pipes, plus we lived in the 1st floor which was not that far from the ground. What made me curious was what she was doing out there and how she learned to go out through the kitchen window. Soon, she had even started bringing food such as meat and would eat them. This behaviour went on, over and over again. She would even not come back at home for a day or for a long period of time then would come back looking very dirty and she hated baths just like the other cats, she would cling the bathroom bars by the window where she tried to escape as she was poured water to rinse her from the soap. Then, on one ordinary day, she left and never came back. Some of my friends knew where she goes and always fed her when she went to them, so we had to let her go because we had tried. But luckily, before she went, she had left us with three kittens. Which we welcomed with great love and care.

Peanut was a very jovial, funny and an adorable cat.
